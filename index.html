About Sala Company

Sala Company makes free web apps that run right in your browser: a private AI chat that runs on your own device, a live world news feed, market heatmaps for crypto, currencies, metals, energy and government bonds, and browser games — from vibe coded arcade classics to Rizq, a roguelike set in Kuwait. Nothing to install and no account to create. Almost everything here was vibe coded from the start: built by describing it to an AI and refining it together, rather than written line by line.

Is everything free?

Yes. Every game and tool here is free to use in any modern browser, on desktop or phone. Only the online store sells anything.

How can an AI chat run privately in the browser?

WebAI downloads an open model such as Llama, Qwen or Gemma once and runs it on your own graphics chip through WebGPU. Your messages are processed on your device and never sent to a server.

Where does Sala News get its headlines?

Sala News reads about 95 public news, government, OSINT and disaster feeds, plus public Bluesky and Telegram channels and 29 news channels on YouTube, and rebuilds the feed every few minutes.

Where does the market data in Heat come from?

Heat uses CoinGecko and exchange candles for crypto, official ECB rates for currencies, Swissquote for precious metals, futures markets for oil and gas, and the official publications of central banks and treasuries for bond yield curves. It is a work in progress and not financial advice.
