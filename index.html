    About Sala Company

    Sala Company makes free web apps that run right in your browser: a live world news feed, market heatmaps for crypto, currencies, metals, energy, US stock ETFs and government bonds, browser games from vibe coded arcade classics to Rizq, a roguelike set in Kuwait, a private AI chat that runs on your own device, How, a picture book that draws how any thing works, Scene to Story, which writes a story from a one-minute video, Artograph, a Spirograph drawing toy, video.sala.company, free live TV channels in a retro TV set, and radio.sala.company, free internet radio in a retro shortwave radio. Nothing to install, and no account to create (except How and Scene to Story, which use your own OpenRouter account for the AI). Almost everything here was vibe coded from the start: built by describing it to an AI and refining it together, rather than written line by line.

    Is everything free?

    Yes. Every game and tool here is free to use in any modern browser, on desktop or phone. Only the store sells anything. The one exception is the AI behind How and Scene to Story, which you pay for through your own OpenRouter account, usually a few cents a page or a story. Sala Company charges nothing for it, and every answer already in How's gallery opens free.

    How does How draw how things work?

    How sends your question to Claude, which works out the parts and the steps and writes a description of the picture; OpenAI's image model then paints one exploded view with each part numbered, and a cheap vision model checks the numbers landed on the right parts. Photos of the real thing come from Wikipedia and the 3D model from Sketchfab. Every page is kept by a small link service so it can be shared and reopened by anyone, with no sign-in and no AI.

    How does Scene to Story turn a video into a story?

    Scene to Story takes still frames from your clip and turns the speech into text on your own device, so the video never leaves it. It then sends only those frames and the transcript to Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, which describes the scene and writes it up as prose, a poem, a screenplay or a fairy tale. It writes in the language of the video, or one you choose, and lays out right-to-left languages such as Arabic properly.

    Where does Sala News get its headlines?

    Sala News reads about 95 public news, government, OSINT and disaster feeds, plus public Bluesky and Telegram channels and 29 news channels on YouTube, and rebuilds the feed every few minutes.

    Where does the market data in Heat come from?

    Heat uses CoinGecko and exchange candles for crypto, official ECB rates for currencies, Swissquote for precious metals, futures markets for oil and gas, Yahoo Finance for US stock ETFs, and the official publications of central banks and treasuries for bond yield curves. It is a work in progress and not financial advice.

    How can an AI chat run privately in the browser?

    WebAI downloads an open model such as Llama, Qwen or Gemma once and runs it on your own graphics chip through WebGPU. Your messages are processed on your device and never sent to a server.
